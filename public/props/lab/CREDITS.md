# Lab props: credits

(public domain, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; credit is given
with thanks. Each was decimated, re-unwrapped and re-baked into one packed texture with the lowpix converter
(`lowpix convert polyhaven:<id> --style realistic --atlas 512`), scaled to its real size; the texture is packed in the .glb.
