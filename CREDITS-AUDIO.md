# Audio credits

Every sound effect, ambience bed and music track in Limbo Phobia is a recorded or produced sound from
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication).
CC0 does not require attribution; the authors are credited here with thanks.

The files in `src/assets/sfx/` were cut, trimmed, loudness-matched, made seamless (loops) and
re-encoded (mono MP3) from the packs below. `src/audio/sampleManifest.ts` maps the game's sound
keys to them. A handful of keys (`piano`, `tinnitus`, the four `vx_whistle_*` tunes, the retired
generative-music instruments and `arv_inside`) still fall back to the old runtime synthesis.
