# Limbo Phobia: Privacy

**Limbo Phobia collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, telemetry or crash
uploads. The game never contacts a server of its own.

What stays on your computer:

- **Saves and settings**: in the game's user-data folder (Windows `%APPDATA%\Limbo Phobia`, Linux
  `~/.config/Limbo Phobia`, macOS `~/Library/Application Support/Limbo Phobia`) and in the browser's local storage.
- **Error log**: `logs/` in the same folder. It records errors and crashes so you can attach it to a bug report
  if you choose. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Photos** from photo mode: your Pictures folder (`Pictures/Limbo Phobia`).

Third parties:

- **Steam** (if you play through Steam): Valve's Steamworks handles achievements, the overlay and Steam Cloud
  (your save folder is synced to your own Steam account). Valve's privacy policy applies to that.
- **Co-op** (optional): connects to the relay server address you type in, and to the other players in your room
  (proximity voice uses WebRTC peer-to-peer and asks for the microphone first). Nothing else goes online.
